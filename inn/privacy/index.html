Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Inn is an app for running a hotel, provided by Kanapan Sangsai (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal data Inn collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, Google shares with us:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your profile photo
  • an identifier for your Google account

Our sign-in provider also keeps security records of each sign-in, such as the time, your IP address and the app version.

How we use it

  • to sign you in and keep you signed in
  • to show your name and photo in your account
  • to protect the service from misuse

We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Where it’s stored

Your account is stored with Supabase, which runs Inn’s servers in Singapore. Sign-in itself is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We share your data with these providers only to run Inn.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Security records are kept by our provider for a limited time and then removed.

Deleting your account

In the app, open your account and tap Delete Account. Your account and its data are deleted right away and can’t be recovered. You can also ask us by email.

Your rights

Under Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act, you can ask to see, correct, or delete your personal data, to get a copy of it, or to object to how we use it. Email us and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Children

Inn is made for hotel businesses and isn’t meant for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
